# 2026-10-29 (13) — Roadmap 0012: one suppression grammar, prefiltered

## What the four copies did

arcleak, deadwood and dolly carried the same grammar and table; fcheck a reimplementation with an ordered rule array and
`disable`/`enable` kind spellings. Differences that matter for compatibility: deadwood trims spaces and tabs only (a
multi-line block comment is not a directive there, it is elsewhere), rule sets versus fcheck's ordered array, and each
tool's Codable shape, which reaches caches and JSON. The shared component parameterises the first and leaves the other
two to tool-owned facades.

## Red, then green

Shared red `2037b9c`: four prefix pairs through every verb, `all`/empty/unknown rules, reasons containing `--`, doc
comments and prose not directives, CRLF and multi-byte offsets, EOF trivia, region switches that do not nest, same-line
order, the double-optional reason. The counting seam: a source with eight comments, three of which carry `@` + a known
namespace; the parser must be called three times. It failed against a stub; green `32cb93e`. Then a boundary test over
0..<130 leading bytes, multi-byte lead, and partial namespaces (`@`, `@a`, `@al`, `@alx:`), green with `a9ed00e`.

The filter: AemiKernels' runtime-dispatched `ByteSet` scan for `@`, then a byte compare of each namespace. It is a
necessary condition, not validation: prose containing `@al:accept` passes the filter and the grammar still rejects it.
Comments without the marker never allocate a substring and never ask the location converter for their line, which is
where the old sweep spent most of its time.

## Adoption without changing a byte

Characterization tests in each tool first (wire keys, kind encoding, duplicate and unknown rules, precedence), green on
the old code, committed alone. Then each tool delegates grammar, sweep and table; types stay as they were. Golden corpus
with all four prefixes: findings and suppressed entries in every tool, JSON and exit codes byte-identical before and
after. Fixture corpora of arcleak, deadwood and dolly against the earlier release binaries: byte-identical. Full suites
green: 213, 371, 196, 75 and analyzerkit's.

## Measured

Debug, first impression only: 0.683 s to 0.409 s. Release, interleaved, five rounds, legacy code copied verbatim into
a throwaway test (kept as `research/probes/SuppressionScanProbe.swift`): 0.284 s to 0.0027 s median on 20,000 comments,
20 directives, outputs asserted equal. ADR-0016 cited the directive parser at about 55 % of arcleak's parse time; the
end-to-end effect on maintastic is not measured here and belongs to 0029.
