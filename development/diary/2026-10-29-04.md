Two assumptions of the plan did not hold. Libraries that do not track `Package.resolved` have nothing to commit; their CI
resolves fresh and already sees 1.7.5. And a consumer never reads a dependency's `Package.resolved`, so aemi's new
resolution needed no new aemi pin downstream: the re-pin chain was unnecessary.

The benchmark packages had drifted below their own manifests (package-benchmark 1.36 resolved against `from: "1.38.0"`,
swift-syntax 603, dolly's swift-system 1.7.5) and predated analyzerkit. They depend on their tool through
`branch: "main"`, so they measure what is pushed, never the change under work; recorded in 0029.

## AemiJSON's failing test

`escapedKeysDoNotAllocatePerKey`: 2,010 allocations for 500 plain keys, 4,011 for 500 escaped ones, budget plain + 8. Pinned
back to swift-collections 1.7.3 it fails identically, so it is not the refresh. Entry 0039.

## CI had stopped running

To see whether AemiJSON's CI knew of that failure, its runs were listed: the last one failed. So had the last run of every
caller of aemi's shared workflows (AemiJSON, AemiRegex, analyzerkit, the five tools, Atelier), all since the evening of
2026-10-28, all with no job started. The run page gives the reason: "error parsing called workflow … swift-tool.yml@afc1be3
…: workflow was not found". aemi's authorship rewrite that day left the pinned commits (`afc1be3`, `4f270e0`) on no branch;
the API still serves them by SHA, but a reusable workflow must be reachable. arcleak's run at 10:13 that morning, same pin,
was green.

The workflow files at the old pins are byte-identical to those at `f6da05c` (aemi `main`), so ten `uses:` lines in nine
repositories now point there, `actionlint` clean before and after (0037). Not pushed, so not yet proven on GitHub. Keeping
it from recurring is 0038.

AemiProtobuf's CI is red for another reason: its own workflow runs on `macos-26` with Swift 6.3.3 while the package needs
6.4, and `swift-format` is missing; it has never been green (0040). 0017 now waits on it.
