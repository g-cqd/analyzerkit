# 2026-10-30 (02) — Step 1 of the revised schedule: what swift-search needs

Six entries, each red first: 0050 `SourcePath.absolute`, 0056 `ExitStatus.noMatch` and `SearchOutcome`, 0053
`IndexFreshness`, 0052 sourcekit-lsp beside libIndexStore, 0051 `LineIndex`, 0049 `family-pins.sh`.

Three things were not what the records assumed:

- `SourcePath.canonical` already makes a relative path absolute against the current directory, because
  `URL(fileURLWithPath:)` does. swift-search's extension was a second spelling of it. What the tools lacked was a
  directory other than the current one.
- `noMatch` cannot be an enum case: Swift refuses two cases with one raw value. It is a documented static alias, and
  `allCases` keeps the sysexits list consumers read.
- The pins script reads working-tree manifests, so it reports the next train, not today's graph. Probe C6 showed that a
  root's own requirement does not override two dependencies that disagree. Its first real run lists the train's work:
  aemi, AemiJSON and AemiRegex each have two spellings across the family, and AemiProtobuf has to move first.

Measured: `LineIndex` builds 10,000 lines (472 KB) in 0.35 ms, against 11.1 ms for swift-search's `TextPositions`, and
converts 2.4 to 8.5 times faster. The freshness memo turns 1,000 questions on 10 files into 10 lookups.

together) in the same checkout, and ask pins it. The repositories are shared: fetch and check the status before
every commit.
