# 2026-10-31 — versions bumped everywhere (0031)

The owner asked for every version reference to move to wave 2. The live ones were few: arcleak's SwiftPM snippet,
analyzerkit's (still `from: "0.1.0"`, now 0.4.0, a new tag for wave 2's sources), ask's ADR-0022, a stale comment in
AemiJSON's CI, swift-search's analyzerkit pin, and maintastic. Diaries, reviews and progress notes name the versions
of their day and were left alone.

maintastic was two waves and a patch behind. Comparing old and new binaries on it with Danger's exact arguments found
one trap: 0.13 and 0.14 treat a `facts` *file* at `--cache-path` as not a cache, and keep it, so a runner that kept its
`ANALYZER_CACHE_PATH` would have run without a cache from then on, silently. Danger now passes `<tool>/cache`.
