# 2026-10-31 — per-file symbol facts extracted (0055); IndexQueries closed (0054)

`SymbolFacts` is in analyzerkit: deadwood's collectors, plus where a name is, the later components of a member type,
and macro declarations. The protobuf messages keep deadwood's field numbers. Its compact references therefore read
as full ones with column 0 and the file scope, and a cached payload needs no translation.

deadwood moved onto it and its Sources lost 2,451 lines net: the collectors, the models and the JSON cache coding that protobuf
had already replaced. Its reports are byte-equal on maintastic with and without the index. The cache grew 1.9%.

swift-search did not move. Read field by field, its output differs from deadwood's in eleven more ways than the
research's three. Several are choices (where a range ends, which keywords count) and not gaps. Each needs a red test
before a switch can be byte-equal, so it is an entry of its own: roadmap 0061.

0054 is closed as well: arcleak, deadwood and swift-search all read the index through `IndexQueries`.
