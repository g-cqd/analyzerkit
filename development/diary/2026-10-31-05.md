# 2026-10-31 — fcheck and modeler on maintastic (0062 to 0066)

The first run of the two newest tools on an app rather than on a library. Both take about a second on 2,530 files and
need no configuration beyond the excludes the other three use there.

fcheck's rules are about descriptors, renames and byte cuts, which an app barely has: one tracked `open`, no
`rename`. Its one finding is real. What an app does have is calls paired on a receiver, capture sessions and locks,
and one of those is wrong in maintastic's QR scanner (0063).

modeler's reentrancy rule fits an app full of `@MainActor` view models and actors (377 and 16). Of its 29 findings,
the real ones are a crash path in the chat (0064 makes it its own rule), stale pagination responses, and a recorder
that can get stuck after an interruption. Five were handled in ways modeler could not see, and are now seen (0062): a
helper that re-checks, a generation token, a cancellation, and a lock on a local. A continuation overwritten in
`Camera` was found by reading the continuation sites, not by either tool (0065).

The TLA+ path was the weakest part: TLC's trace began from a state the code refuses, because a guard on a computed
property sets no precondition in the model. `check` and `states` carried the value here.
