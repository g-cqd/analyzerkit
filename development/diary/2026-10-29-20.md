# 2026-10-29 (20) — Roadmap 0015: the interner, measured, then built

## Inputs that are the tools' own

The probe interned what deadwood and dolly intern on maintastic, dumped by throwaway tests in deadwood: every USR of the
index store in occurrence order (from a copy of the store in `/tmp`, since reading one creates an `IndexDatabase` beside it,
and the DerivedData folder is Xcode's), and every token SwiftParser gives for the Swift files. ADR-0012's 73,319 symbols
were the project's declarations; the store's occurrences reach 120,256 distinct USRs with the SDK's.

## What decided it

Numbers in aemi's diary 2026-10-29-03. The arena interner is five times the dictionary on USRs and 2.5 times on tokens,
mostly because it never makes a `String`; wyhash wins on both; the merge beats a lock-striped table at every worker count
and is the only one of the two that is deterministic.

The probe had a bug that looked like a finding: wyhash interned tokens slowly although it hashed them fastest. Its seed was
zero, so short keys hashed by length. Checked against the reference `wyhash.h` afterwards, which also caught an off-by-one
at 48 bytes. Every hash number in the records comes from the corrected port, and the same reference values are now aemi's
tests.

## Built, and where it is slower than the probe

Red then green in aemi. The package benchmark is about 20% slower on one thread than the probe; the same sources compiled
into one module with the probe's harness equal the probe, so it is the call into another module. Left for the consumers to
measure rather than exposing the table with `@inlinable` now.

Two things about tests, both now in aemi's diary: the first allocation guard measured its own loop (debug builds allocate
iterating an array), and every exit test in aemi aborts under ThreadSanitizer, an existing condition.
